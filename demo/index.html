The policy behind the trace

Bound the work
Up to 3 attempts per read, a 200 ms timeout per attempt and a 25 ms delay between retries.
Stop repeated dependency calls
Two exhausted reads open the circuit for 30 seconds. A half-open circuit admits one probe, with no retry.
Keep cancellation meaningful
Caller cancellation is not retried. Only transient read failures and timeouts use the retry budget.
